spider-man

Real name: Most famously Peter Parker, though other Spider-People include Miles Morales, Gwen Stacy (Spider-Gwen), and others in the multiverse. Powers: Superhuman strength, agility, wall-crawling, a "Spider-Sense" that warns of danger, and web-shooters (usually inventions by Peter Parker). Famous quote: "With great power comes great responsibility." Home: New York City. Main villains: Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, Venom, Sandman, Electro, Mysterio, and the Lizard.
